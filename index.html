Questions, answered

Is this free?

Yes. The channel is free to follow while we build a real, auditable track record. Nothing is sold until the record proves itself.

How can I trust the record?

Every signal is written to an append-only journal and bound into a SHA-256 chain. The published chain head is verifiable: anyone can re-run our open tool on the raw data and check it still matches. If a single entry were edited or dropped, every later hash changes.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are decision-support signals with clear risk levels. We never guarantee profit and losses are part of the public record. Trade decisions are always yours.

What markets do you cover?

Gold (XAUUSD), silver (XAGUSD) and the Nasdaq 100 (NDX100), using H1 trend signals with defined stop and target levels.

Do I need the bot?

No — signals post straight to the channel. The bot adds a guided welcome (type /start) and explains the signal format (/signals).
